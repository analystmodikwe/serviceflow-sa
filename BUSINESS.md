# ServiceFlow SA

> **One-line pitch:** ServiceFlow SA gives independent service providers a simple
> booking link so clients can book instantly, and providers stop losing money to
> double-bookings, no-shows and buried WhatsApp messages.

## 1. The problem
Millions of South Africans earn a living as independent service providers:
barbers, braiders, nail techs, mobile car washers, tutors and more. Most take
bookings through WhatsApp chats, phone calls and paper diaries. This causes:
- Double-bookings and forgotten appointments
- No-shows with no way to follow up
- Hours lost to back-and-forth about prices and availability
- No record of clients or income
Clients also can't see when a provider is free without messaging them.

[Add a real stat or a short story from someone you know who runs bookings this way.]

## 2. The customer
**Primary:** independent and home-based providers (1 to 3 people) who book
clients by phone or WhatsApp.
**Secondary:** their clients, who want to book quickly from a phone.
**Launch focus:** beauty and grooming (barbers, braiders, nail techs).

## 3. The solution
Providers sign up, list their services (name, price, duration) and set weekly
availability. They share a personal booking link on WhatsApp, Instagram or a
business card. Clients pick a service and an open slot and book in seconds.
The system prevents double-booking, and providers confirm or cancel from one
dashboard.

## 4. Impact
- Fewer no-shows and double-bookings
- Hours saved each week on admin messages
- A client list and booking history for each provider
- Clients get 24/7 booking without waiting for a reply

## 6. Go-to-market
1. Start with beauty and grooming providers in [your area].
2. Onboard providers in person (salons, barber shops, home-based providers) and
   through local WhatsApp and Instagram communities.
3. Each provider shares their booking link with their clients, which brings
   in more clients and more word of mouth.
4. Expand to mobile and home services (car wash, tutoring, cleaning).

## 7. Competitors
| Competitor | What they do | Gap ServiceFlow SA fills |
|---|---|---|
| Fresha | Salon booking, payments, marketing | Built for salons and shops; heavy for solo providers |
| Booksy | All-in-one booking and business management | Paid plan [verify price]; salon and barber focus |
| Haircut Now | Free barber booking | Barbers only |
| WhatsApp and paper diaries | What most providers use today | No availability view, no reminders, easy to double-book |

**Our edge:** simple, mobile-first, free to start, and built for any independent
service provider, including those who work at the client's location.
